Anna orders lunch for the office every day. Each delivery comes with some bags of utensils and each bag has a fork, a spoon and a knife for one person. In theory, the restaurant sends one bag for every person eating. In practice, the kitchen seems to decide the number of bags with a dice roll, so some days there are fewer. 

Anna hates waste. When a delivery driver brings more bags than there are people at lunch, she puts the extra bags in a drawer that serves as the office reserve. The drawer never fills up: it holds every bag Anna puts in it. When a delivery brings too few bags, the people left without one take a bag from the reserve, for as long as it lasts. If the reserve runs out and someone is still staring at their food without a fork. Anna walks to the store and buys exactly as many bags as are still missing. The bags she buys are used right away so none of them end up in the reserve. 

Anna has ordered lunch for *n* days, and the reserve was initially empty. Given the number of bags and the number of people on each day, how many bags did Anna buy at the store in total.
